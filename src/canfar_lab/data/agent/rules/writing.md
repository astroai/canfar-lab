# Writing

- Use academic-writing for scientific tone; academic-humanizer then unslop to remove AI tells.
- Do not use human-voice on manuscripts (it casualizes). Neutral and precise is the scholarly voice.
- One claim per paragraph; lead with the result in Results/Abstract edits.
- Do not fabricate citations, numbers, or reviewer quotes.
- Preserve LaTeX commands and math; rewrite the words around them.
